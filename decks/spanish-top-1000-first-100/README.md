# Spanish Top 1000: first 100 words (free sample)

Ranks 1–100 of the paid [Spanish Top 1000 Most Common Words](https://ankiwordbank.com/decks/spanish-top-1000-most-common-words)
deck (rebuilt October 27, 2026), cut from the package customers download. The note
type, note IDs and deck name are unchanged, so importing the full deck later fills in
this same deck and keeps your progress.

- **File:** [`spanish-top-1000-first-100.apkg`](spanish-top-1000-first-100.apkg)
- **Every card:** [`words.tsv`](words.tsv) (rank, word, Spanish sentence, English, audio file)
- **The full ranked list:** [`spanish-top-1000-words.tsv`](spanish-top-1000-words.tsv), all 1,000
  headwords with their frequency per million words and the forms that were counted.
- **Notes:** 100, one card each. All 100 have audio.
- **Cards:** English sentence on the front; Spanish sentence and audio on the back. The target word is in bold on 100 of 100 cards.
- **Word list:** the 1,000 most frequent words in the
  [Wiktionary Spanish frequency list](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Frequency_lists/Spanish1000)
  (Matthias Buchmeier's 27.4-million-word film and TV subtitle corpus, CC BY-SA), grouped by
  dictionary form (*es, era, son, soy* all count for *ser*), excluding names, interjections and swear words.
- **Audio:** four ElevenLabs AI voices, each cloned from a native speaker from Spain. AI-generated, not human recordings.
