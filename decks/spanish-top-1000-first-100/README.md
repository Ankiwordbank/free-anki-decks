# Spanish Top 1000: first 100 words (free sample)

Ranks 1–100 of the paid [Spanish Top 1000 Most Common Words](https://ankiwordbank.com/decks/spanish-top-1000-most-common-words)
deck, cut from the package customers download. The note type, note IDs and deck
name are unchanged, so importing the full deck later fills in this same deck and
keeps your progress.

- **File:** [`spanish-top-1000-first-100.apkg`](spanish-top-1000-first-100.apkg)
- **Every card:** [`words.tsv`](words.tsv) (rank, word, Spanish sentence, English, audio file)
- **Notes:** 100, one card each. All 100 have audio.
- **Cards:** English sentence on the front; Spanish sentence and audio on the back. The target word is in bold on 89 of 100 cards.
- **Word list:** ranked by frequency, starting *ser, y, a, en, la, de, que*. The source list was not recorded when the deck was built (December 2025).
- **Audio:** neural text-to-speech (synthetic voice).
