# German verbs: 50 common verbs in example sentences

Free deck, identical to the file on [ankiwordbank.com](https://ankiwordbank.com/decks/german-verbs-made-easy-50-common-verbs-audio).

- **File:** [`german-verbs-50.apkg`](german-verbs-50.apkg)
- **Every card:** [`words.tsv`](words.tsv)
- **Notes:** 50, one per verb. All 50 have audio.
- **Cards:** English sentence on the front; German sentence with the verb in bold, plus audio, on the back.
- **Audio:** neural text-to-speech (synthetic voice).

## Known issues

- #33 (*treffen*): "Lass uns morgen im Park treffen" is missing the reflexive *uns*; it should be "Lass uns **uns** morgen im Park treffen". The short form is common in speech but not standard.
- #27 (*stehen*): "einfach nur stehen und entspannen" ("just stand and relax") is an odd thing to say.
- #1 (*gehen*): "ins Kino mit Freunden" is understandable but less natural than "mit Freunden ins Kino".

This deck has not had a native-speaker review.
