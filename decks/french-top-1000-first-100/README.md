# French Top 1000: first 100 words (free sample)

Ranks 1–100 of the paid [French Top 1000 Most Common Words](https://ankiwordbank.com/decks/french-complete-1000-most-common-words-audio)
deck (rebuilt October 27, 2026), cut from the package customers download. The note
type, note IDs and deck name are unchanged, so importing the full deck later fills in
this same deck and keeps your progress.

- **File:** [`french-top-1000-first-100.apkg`](french-top-1000-first-100.apkg)
- **Every card:** [`words.tsv`](words.tsv) (rank, word, French sentence, English, audio file)
- **The full ranked list:** [`french-top-1000-words.tsv`](french-top-1000-words.tsv), all 1,000
  headwords with their frequency per million words and the forms that were counted.
- **Notes:** 100, one card each. All 100 have audio.
- **Cards:** English sentence on the front; French sentence and audio on the back. The target word is in bold on 100 of 100 cards.
- **Word list:** the 1,000 most frequent words in [Lexique 3.83](http://www.lexique.org/)'s
  film-subtitle frequencies (`freqlemfilms2`, CC BY-SA 4.0), grouped by dictionary form
  (*est, était, sont, suis* all count for *être*), excluding names, interjections and swear words.
- **Audio:** four ElevenLabs AI voices, each cloned from a native speaker from France. AI-generated, not human recordings.
