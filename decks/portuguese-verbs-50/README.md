# Portuguese verbs: 30 core verbs and 24 verb phrases

Free deck in Brazilian Portuguese (*você*, *lição de casa*), identical to the file on [ankiwordbank.com](https://ankiwordbank.com/decks/portuguese-verbs-50-common-verbs-examples-audio).

- **File:** [`portuguese-verbs-50.apkg`](portuguese-verbs-50.apkg)
- **Every card:** [`words.tsv`](words.tsv)
- **Notes:** 54. Notes 1–30 are single verbs (*fazer, dar, ser, ter…*); notes 31–54 are everyday verb phrases (*jogar futebol, tomar banho, fazer compras*). All 54 have audio.
- **Cards:** English sentence on the front; Portuguese sentence with the verb in bold, plus audio, on the back.
- **Audio:** neural text-to-speech (synthetic voice).

## Known issues

- #11 (*vir*): "Eu vou vir à sua festa" is understandable, but most speakers would say "Eu vou à sua festa".
